## SERVICES

### 01 — Custom Turbine Design
- Full concept-to-detail turbine design: rotor, drivetrain, tower, and control system architecture engineered as one integrated system, not siloed components.
- Aero-elastic modelling and dynamic design analysis performed against site-specific wind classes and IEC 61400-1 design load cases.
- Design iteration backed by fatigue life and ultimate limit state calculations, so concepts arrive at certification review pre-hardened, not pre-optimistic.

### 02 — Advanced Loads & Structural Analysis
- Integrated load calculations covering turbine, tower, and support structure — including wind, wave, and soil-structure interaction for offshore foundations.
- Fatigue assessment and stress concentration factor (SCF) analysis on welded and grouted connections, run to ultimate and serviceability limit states.
- Load verification and design basis documentation structured for direct submission to certification bodies — built to be checked, not just believed.

### 03 — Wind Resource & Site Assessment
- Site-specific wind and metocean data analysis to derive design-basis conditions — wind shear, turbulence, wave climate, seabed and soil parameters.
- Energy yield modelling with P50/P90 exceedance probability outputs for financing and investment decisions.
- Feasibility and FEED-level studies that pressure-test site viability before capital commitments are made.

### 04 — Project Development & Owner's Engineering
- Independent Owner's Engineer representation across initiation, development, engineering, and construction — coordinating designers, contractors, and certifiers under one technical line of sight.
- Technical due diligence for investors and developers: design basis review, load process audit, and third-party opinion on structural and commercial risk.
- Structural integrity management and performance monitoring through construction and into early operation.

### 05 — Type Certification Support
- Design documentation, load case verification, and technical file preparation aligned to IEC 61400-1 and IECRE OD-501/OD-502 schemes.
- Prototype test planning and evidence packages structured to withstand independent design assessment.
- Direct engineering liaison with certification bodies to close review comments fast — we speak their language because we build to their standards.

### 06 — Certification Body Support
- Independent design review and load verification services for certification bodies needing deep bench strength on complex or contested files.
- Site-specific design assessment support, including foundation and support structure verification to BSH-level rigour for German offshore waters.
- Grid code compliance review and documentation, helping certifiers and developers navigate compliance complexity without slowing project timelines.

### 07 — R&D Center Outreach
- Fully outsourced or co-located R&D capacity — a dedicated senior engineering team working exclusively against a client's product roadmap.
- Coverage across turbine architecture, structural analysis, and certification strategy, scaled up or down as programme phases demand.
- Direct access to the same engineers across a programme's life — continuity that in-house teams lose to attrition and staffing benches lose to rotation.

## WHY ALDOTT

**H2:** Independent counsel. Senior hands. Full lifecycle.

Wind energy has no shortage of engineering vendors. It has a shortage of firms that will tell a client the uncomfortable technical truth — and then stay on the project long enough to fix it.

**Independence of advice**
Aldott is not owned by a turbine OEM, a developer, or a certification body. Our technical recommendations — on load margins, foundation concepts, or certification readiness — are not shaped by a parent company's commercial interest. That independence is why OEMs, developers, and certifiers all bring us the same file without conflict.

**Lifecycle framing, not project framing**
We think in terms of initiation, development, engineering, construction, and operation — not in terms of billable deliverables. A loads model we build during concept design is the same model we defend during certification and revisit during operational performance review. Nothing gets re-engineered from scratch because nothing was ever handed off.

**Senior access, not account layers**
Every Aldott engagement is led by the engineers who will actually run the numbers — not a client-facing lead backed by a junior bench. At 150+ projects and counting, that discipline is the reason clients return for the next phase instead of re-tendering.

**Built to certification-grade rigour**
Our documentation, load verification, and design basis work are structured to the same standard a certification body would apply to itself — IEC 61400 series alignment, IECRE scheme logic, and BSH-level rigour for German offshore work. We build files that survive scrutiny, because we've sat on both sides of the review table.

**Leaner than the large certification houses. Deeper than a staffing bench.**
DNV and UL Solutions set the bar for independent technical authority at scale. Aldott operates at the scale a project actually needs — senior engineering depth without the queue, full lifecycle coverage without the overhead of a global certification body.
